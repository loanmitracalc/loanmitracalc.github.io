Car Loan EMI Calculator

Our Car Loan EMI Calculator helps you calculate your estimated monthly car loan EMI, total interest, total payment and interest breakdown. Simply enter the car loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure to get your estimated repayment details.

How Does a Car Loan EMI Calculator Work?

Car loan EMI depends mainly on the principal loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment tenure. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly EMI, but it may increase the total interest paid over the complete loan period.

Car Loan EMI Formula

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)N ÷ ((1 + R)N − 1)

Here, P represents the principal loan amount, R represents the monthly interest rate and N represents the total number of monthly instalments.

Example of Car Loan EMI

For example, if you take a car loan of ₹8,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 9% for 5 years, this calculator will estimate the monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment amount.

Benefits of Using a Car Loan Calculator

A car loan calculator can help you compare different loan amounts, interest rates and repayment periods before applying for a car loan. It can also help you understand the approximate monthly EMI and total cost of borrowing.

Important Note

The results provided by this calculator are estimates for informational purposes only. Actual EMI, interest rate, processing fees, insurance, taxes and other charges may vary depending on the lender and loan terms. Please check the final repayment schedule provided by your lender.
