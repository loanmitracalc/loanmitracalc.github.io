Car Loan EMI Calculator

Our Car Loan EMI Calculator helps you estimate your monthly car loan EMI, total interest, interest breakdown and total repayment amount. Enter the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure to calculate your estimated EMI quickly.

How Does a Car Loan EMI Calculator Work?

Car loan EMI depends on the loan amount, annual interest rate and loan tenure. A longer repayment period may reduce the monthly EMI, but it can increase the total interest payable.

Car Loan EMI Formula

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)N ÷ ((1 + R)N − 1)

Here, P is the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate and N is the total number of monthly instalments.

Example

For example, if you take a car loan of ₹8,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 9% for 5 years, this calculator estimates your monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment amount.
