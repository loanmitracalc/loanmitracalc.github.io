Example / How to Understand

Suppose the Bevel Protractor reading is:

Main Scale Reading = 1°
Main Minute Scale Reading = 5′
Base Reading = 1° 5′
Least Count = 5′

5′ Least Count means the instrument reading is taken in 5-minute steps:

0′, 5′, 10′, 15′, 20′, 25′, 30′, 35′, 40′, 45′, 50′, 55′

Important: 1° = 60′. When the minute reading reaches 60′, it becomes the next degree.

5′ Least Count:
Valid minute readings are: 0′, 5′, 10′, 15′, 20′, 25′, 30′, 35′, 40′, 45′, 50′ and 55′.

Example:
11° 55′ + 5′ = 12° 0′

11° 0′ − 5′ = 10° 55′
