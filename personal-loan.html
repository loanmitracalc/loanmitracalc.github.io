Personal Loan EMI Calculator

LoanMitraCalc Personal Loan EMI Calculator helps you calculate your monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment amount quickly and easily.

How to use the calculator?

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter your loan tenure.
  4. Select Years, Months or Days.
  5. Click on Calculate EMI.
  6. After calculation, click Download PDF to save your result.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)N / [(1 + R)N − 1]

Where P is the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate and N is the number of monthly installments.
