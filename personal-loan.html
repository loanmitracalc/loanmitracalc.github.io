Personal Loan EMI Calculator

Our Personal Loan EMI Calculator helps you estimate your loan repayment, interest and EMI based on the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure. You can select interest rate per year, month or day and choose loan tenure in years, months or days.

Personal Loan Interest Calculation

The calculator provides an estimated interest amount for the selected loan period. It also displays the estimated interest per year, month and day for easier understanding.

Loan Tenure Options

You can select loan tenure in years, months or days. For years or months, the calculator displays an estimated Monthly EMI. When days are selected, it displays an estimated Daily EMI.

How to Use Personal Loan Calculator

Enter the loan amount, select the interest rate type, enter the interest rate and choose the loan tenure. Then click the Calculate EMI button to view the estimated repayment details.

Important Note

The results shown by this calculator are estimates for informational purposes only. Actual loan interest, EMI, processing fees, charges and repayment terms may vary depending on the lender and loan agreement. Year, month and day conversions use standard approximate periods.
