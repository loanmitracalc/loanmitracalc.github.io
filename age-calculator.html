Age Calculator

Our Age Calculator helps you calculate your exact age in years, months and days. Enter your date of birth to find your current age quickly and easily.

How Does an Age Calculator Work?

An age calculator calculates the difference between your date of birth and the selected current date. It can show your age in complete years, months and days.

How to Calculate Your Age?

To calculate your age, enter your date of birth and use the current date. The calculator determines the difference between these two dates and provides your estimated age.

Example

For example, if your date of birth is 15 September 1996, the calculator can calculate your age in years, months and days based on the current date.

Age Calculator Uses

An age calculator can be useful for checking your current age, calculating age for forms and applications, and understanding the exact time between two dates.
