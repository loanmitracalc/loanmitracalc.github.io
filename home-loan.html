Home Loan EMI Calculator

LoanMitraCalc Home Loan EMI Calculator helps you calculate your estimated monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment amount. Enter your home loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure to get the calculation instantly.

How Does a Home Loan EMI Calculator Work?

Home loan EMI depends mainly on the loan amount, annual interest rate and loan tenure. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly EMI but may increase the total interest payable over the loan period.

Home Loan EMI Formula

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)N ÷ ((1 + R)N − 1)

Here, P is the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate and N is the total number of monthly instalments.

Example

If you borrow ₹30,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 8.5% for 20 years, this calculator can estimate your monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment amount.
