About LoanMitraCalc

LoanMitraCalc is a simple online financial calculator website designed to help users calculate loan EMI, total interest, total payment, SIP investment returns, GST, percentage and age quickly.

How EMI Calculator Works

Enter your loan amount, annual interest rate and loan tenure. Click the Calculate EMI button to see your monthly EMI, total interest and total payment.

These calculations are estimates for informational purposes. Actual loan terms may vary depending on the lender.

Free Online Loan & Financial Calculators

LoanMitraCalc provides free online calculators to help you estimate loan EMIs, investment returns, taxes, percentages and age. You can use these calculators from your mobile, tablet or computer without installing any software.

Loan Calculators

Use our Personal Loan, Home Loan and Car Loan EMI Calculators to estimate your monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment amount. Enter the loan amount, interest rate and tenure to get an estimated result.

Investment Calculator

Our SIP Calculator helps you estimate potential investment growth based on your monthly investment, expected annual return and investment period. Actual investment returns may vary depending on market performance.

GST and Percentage Calculators

Use the GST Calculator to estimate GST amounts and tax-inclusive prices. The Percentage Calculator can be used for common percentage calculations, including percentage increase and decrease.

Age Calculator

Our Age Calculator helps you calculate your age in years, months and days using your date of birth and the selected date.

Bevel Protractor Calculator

The Bevel Protractor Calculator helps calculate angle readings using degrees and minutes. The calculator supports minute readings in 5-minute steps and uses 1 degree = 60 minutes for conversion.
