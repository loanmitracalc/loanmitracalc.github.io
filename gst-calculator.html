GST Calculator

Our GST Calculator helps you quickly calculate GST amount, GST-inclusive price and GST-exclusive price. Enter the amount and GST rate to get an estimated result instantly.

How Does a GST Calculator Work?

A GST calculator calculates the Goods and Services Tax based on the amount and applicable GST rate. It can help you understand the GST amount and the final price of a product or service.

GST Calculation Formula

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

For a GST-inclusive amount, the GST component can be calculated from the total price and applicable GST rate.

Example

For example, if the original price is ₹10,000 and the GST rate is 18%, the GST amount would be ₹1,800 and the total price including GST would be ₹11,800.

Important Note

GST calculator results are provided for informational purposes only. The actual tax applicable may depend on the product, service, applicable GST rate and current tax rules.
