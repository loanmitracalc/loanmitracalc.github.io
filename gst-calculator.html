GST Calculator

LoanMitraCalc GST Calculator is a free online tool that helps you calculate GST amount, CGST, SGST and the final amount quickly and accurately.

How to Calculate GST?

Enter the original amount, select the applicable GST rate and choose whether you want to add GST or remove GST from the given amount.

GST Calculation Formula

When GST is added:

GST Amount = Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Final Amount = Amount + GST Amount

When GST is removed from an inclusive amount:

Original Amount = Inclusive Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)

CGST and SGST

For intra-state transactions, GST is generally divided equally into CGST and SGST. For example, 18% GST consists of 9% CGST and 9% SGST.
