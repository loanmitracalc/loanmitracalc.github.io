GST Calculator

LoanMitraCalc GST Calculator is a free online tool that helps you calculate Goods and Services Tax quickly. Enter the amount, choose the applicable GST rate and select whether you want to add or remove GST.

How Does a GST Calculator Work?

A GST calculator calculates the tax amount based on the original price and applicable GST rate. It can also calculate the GST component from a tax-inclusive amount.

GST Calculation Formula

For adding GST:

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Final Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount

For removing GST from an inclusive amount:

Original Amount = Inclusive Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)

GST Amount = Inclusive Amount − Original Amount

CGST and SGST Calculation

For a standard intra-state transaction, the total GST can be divided equally between CGST and SGST. For example, at an 18% GST rate, CGST is 9% and SGST is 9%.

GST Calculator Example

Suppose the original price is ₹10,000 and the GST rate is 18%. The GST amount is ₹1,800. Therefore, the final price including GST is ₹11,800. CGST is ₹900 and SGST is ₹900.

Important Note

GST calculator results are provided for informational and estimation purposes only. The actual tax applicable may depend on the product or service, transaction type, applicable GST rate and current tax rules.
