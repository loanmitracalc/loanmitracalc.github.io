Percentage Calculator

LoanMitraCalc Percentage Calculator is a free online tool that makes percentage calculations quick and easy. You can calculate a percentage of a number and also see addition, subtraction, multiplication and division results.

How to Calculate Percentage?

Enter the number and percentage value in the calculator and click the Calculate button.

Percentage = Number × Percentage ÷ 100

Example

If the number is 500 and the percentage is 10%:

500 × 10 ÷ 100 = 50

Addition: 500 + 50 = 550

Subtraction: 500 − 50 = 450

Multiplication: 500 × 50 = 25,000

Division: 500 ÷ 50 = 10
